I am a cybersecurity engineer and researcher specialising in **applied cryptography** and **public key infrastructure**, currently working on verifiable fully homomorphic encryption at the [IMDEA Software Institute](https://software.imdea.org/) in Madrid.

I like working at the boundary between research and engineering: taking cryptographic constructions that look good on paper and finding out what it actually takes to run them in real, constrained environments like satellites, hospitals, and banks.

## What I have worked on

- **Verifiable FHE** at the [IMDEA Software Institute](https://software.imdea.org/): verifiable homomorphic encryption with succinct proofs.
- **Space cryptography** at the [European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/): ground-segment cryptographic services and post-quantum PKI for space missions.
- **Privacy-preserving systems** at [TU Delft](https://www.tudelft.nl/): blockchain-based medical data research and encrypted search.
- **Backend & infrastructure engineering**: instant payments at [ING Bank](https://www.ing.ro/), cloud and on-premises platforms as a DevOps engineer.

The details live on the [research](/research/) and [projects](/projects/) pages; education and the full track record are in my [CV](/cv.pdf).
