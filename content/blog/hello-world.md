Title: Why this site exists
Date: 2026-07-04
Slug: why-this-site-exists

I have spent the last few years moving between engineering and research — payment systems at a bank, ground-segment cryptography at a space agency, privacy-preserving platforms in academia, and now verifiable homomorphic encryption at a research institute. Most of what I learned in the gaps between those worlds never got written down anywhere.

This site is meant to fix that. The plan is simple:

- Occasional technical posts about what I am working on — cryptography, PKI, security engineering, and the practical trade-offs that papers tend to leave out.
- No schedule, no filler. I will write when I have something worth saying.

The site itself follows the same philosophy: static HTML generated by a [single Scala script](https://github.com/rosualinpetru/rosualinpetru.github.io/blob/main/build.scala) using [Laika](https://typelevel.org/Laika/) for markdown, a single small stylesheet, no JavaScript, hosted for free on GitHub Pages. A new post is a markdown file and a `git push`.

If any of this sounds interesting, the [blog feed](/feeds/all.atom.xml) is the way to follow along.
